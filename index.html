Core scientific premise: the hazard inversion paradigm
Publication-grade model

Partial oxidation in Advanced Oxidation Processes produces transformation products (TPs) that can be more toxic, bioaccumulative, or hazardous than the original parent contaminant. PlasmaGuard computes precisely when during treatment the water is most hazardous — the hazard inversion window — and computes the definitive run time (t_safe) required for genuine non-toxic discharge into aquatic ecosystems.
